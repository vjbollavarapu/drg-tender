# 📈 SLA Definition — DRG Platform

## 🎯 Purpose

Define service level expectations for platform operations.

---

## 🧱 SLA Components

### 1. Availability

| Service | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Core Platform | 99.5% – 99.9% |

---

### 2. Incident Response

| Severity | Response Time | Resolution Target |
|----------|--------------|------------------|
| Critical | < 15 min | < 4 hours |
| High | < 30 min | < 8 hours |
| Medium | < 2 hours | < 24 hours |
| Low | < 1 day | As scheduled |

---

### 3. Monitoring

- 24x7 monitoring
- Real-time alerts
- Automated escalation

---

### 4. Reporting

- Monthly SLA reports
- Incident reports
- Performance trends

---

### 5. Disaster Recovery

| Metric | Target |
|-------|--------|
| RTO | Defined per system |
| RPO | Defined per system |
