# 💰 Commercial Framework — Platform & Operations Scope (AWS-Aligned)

## 🎯 Purpose

Define a structured, scope-based commercial model for platform engineering and operations services delivered by QK Prima.

---

## 🧠 Principle

> Commercial value is derived from responsibility, risk, and continuity — not infrastructure.

---

## 🧱 Scope-Based Pricing Model

The commercial value reflects responsibility across:

### 1. Platform Architecture & Engineering
- Environment design (Dev/UAT/Prod)
- Deployment model
- Integration architecture

---

### 2. Data Migration & Integration
- Migration planning and execution
- Data validation and reconciliation
- External system integration (SMRP, MyGDX)

---

### 3. Security & Compliance
- RBAC implementation
- Encryption enforcement
- Audit readiness

---

### 4. Observability & Reliability
- Monitoring
- Alerting
- Incident response

---

### 5. SLA & Operations (36 Months)
- Support model (L2/L3)
- Incident management
- Performance monitoring

---

### 6. Disaster Recovery
- DR design
- Failover procedures
- DR testing

---

## 📊 Commercial Band (Guidance)

Based on scope and duration:

| Scope | Estimated Range (MYR) |
|------|----------------------|
| Full Platform Engineering + Operations (36 months) | 900,000 – 1,200,000 |

---

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- This is **not infrastructure cost**
- This excludes:
  - AWS billing
  - Casemix licensing
- This reflects:
  - Platform ownership
  - Operational responsibility
  - Risk coverage

---

## 🎯 Commercial Position

> The commercial model reflects long-term operational responsibility, not one-time implementation effort.

---

## 🚀 Final Statement

> Platform responsibility spans the entire lifecycle, and the commercial model reflects this continuity.
