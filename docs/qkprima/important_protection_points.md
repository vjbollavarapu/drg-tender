# Internal Protection Points Between QK Prima and Our Company

1. Our company should be explicitly recognised as the owner of AWS cloud architecture and operations scope.

2. QK Prima should be explicitly recognised as programme support and coordination partner, not owner of cloud technical scope.

3. Functional defects in the Casemix application should not be attributed to our company unless directly caused by cloud/platform misconfiguration.

4. Any major expansion in scope should trigger a review of internal sharing.

5. If 24x7 support, additional environments, on-site deployment, or government-cloud responsibilities increase materially, the internal split should be revisited.

6. Revenue sharing should be linked to actual payment received upstream, with clarity on invoicing flow and timing.

7. Internal agreement should clearly define who bears which delivery obligations and who interfaces with Casemix for which matters.
