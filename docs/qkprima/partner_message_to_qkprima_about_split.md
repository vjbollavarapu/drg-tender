# Proposed Internal Responsibility and Commercial Sharing Discussion

Based on the current structure where Casemix remains the DRG solution and domain owner, I believe the cleanest model is for QK Prima and our company to jointly support Casemix with clearly separated responsibilities.

## Proposed role split

### QK Prima
- commercial and partner coordination
- programme and meeting coordination
- delivery support and follow-up
- documentation consolidation
- local engagement and implementation coordination
- administrative and PMO-style support
- helpdesk coordination and escalation administration

### Our Company
- AWS cloud architecture
- environment setup and hosting model
- DevSecOps and CI/CD
- cloud security, IAM, encryption and secrets handling
- monitoring, logging and alerting
- backup, disaster recovery and resilience
- deployment governance
- technical incident response and production stability
- testing environment readiness
- cloud operations across the full lifecycle

## Proposed commercial sharing
Since our company will be taking the full specialised cloud and operations responsibility across the 3-year lifecycle, while QK Prima will be handling programme support, commercial coordination and implementation support, my suggested internal split is:

- Our Company: 65%
- QK Prima: 35%

I believe this gives a fair reflection of:
- technical ownership,
- delivery risk,
- long-term continuity responsibility,
- and programme support contribution.

If the actual scope evolves and QK Prima takes a larger operational role, we can revisit the split accordingly.
