# Proposed Responsibility Positioning
## Between Our Company and QK Prima
## In Support of Casemix for the National DRG Programme

## Context
The delivery chain is expected to be structured as follows:

- Government
- Casemix (DRG solution and domain owner)
- QK Prima
- Our Company

In this structure, Casemix remains the principal DRG subject-matter and solution owner, while QK Prima and our company act as the supporting implementation and cloud operations layer.

## Strategic Position
Our recommendation is that QK Prima and our company should present ourselves as a coordinated support and delivery partner layer to Casemix, with clear division of responsibilities to avoid overlap, ambiguity, and delivery risk.

## Proposed Positioning
### Casemix
Casemix remains responsible for:
- DRG subject matter
- application solution ownership
- DRG grouping logic
- costing methodology
- business workflows
- reporting logic
- clinical and casemix domain engagement
- principal tender/solution positioning to Government

### QK Prima
QK Prima should be positioned as:
- programme support partner
- commercial and coordination partner
- local implementation support partner
- documentation and liaison support partner
- client coordination and administrative support layer
- deployment coordination and delivery support layer
- first-line project orchestration support with Casemix and Government-facing coordination where needed

### Our Company
Our company should be positioned as:
- AWS cloud platform engineering partner
- cloud architecture and deployment partner
- DevSecOps and CI/CD partner
- security, IAM, and encryption partner
- observability, monitoring, and incident response partner
- backup, disaster recovery, and resilience partner
- runtime operations and service reliability partner
- technical production-readiness and cloud governance partner

## Why This Split Makes Sense
The tender document clearly expects much more than just application software. It includes:
- cloud-hosted database architecture,
- API and SFTP integrations,
- IAM/RBAC,
- auditability,
- DevSecOps and CI/CD,
- Helpdesk and SLA,
- security and compliance,
- UAT, PAT and FAT,
- and ongoing operational readiness.

That means the platform and operational layer must be owned properly.

QK Prima and our company should therefore divide responsibilities in a way that:
- avoids duplication,
- allows Casemix to focus on DRG domain delivery,
- allows QK Prima to support engagement, delivery coordination, and programme support,
- and allows our company to take responsibility for AWS cloud and operations.
