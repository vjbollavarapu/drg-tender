# Detailed Scope Proposed for QK Prima
## Programme Support, Coordination, Delivery Support and Local Engagement

## 1. Commercial and Partner Coordination
QK Prima can handle:
- commercial coordination with Casemix
- partner communications
- internal delivery coordination
- subcontracting and commercial administration
- invoice and payment coordination
- commercial follow-up with upstream partner

## 2. Programme Coordination
QK Prima can handle:
- project schedule coordination
- meeting management
- action tracking
- workstream coordination
- partner follow-ups
- implementation planning support
- deployment scheduling coordination

## 3. Documentation and Submission Support
QK Prima can handle:
- document consolidation
- version control for submissions
- partner document collation
- programme documentation support
- formatting and submission coordination
- administrative compliance tracking

## 4. Stakeholder Liaison Support
QK Prima can handle:
- coordination with Casemix on requests
- scheduling workshops and review sessions
- follow-up on required information
- communication flow between parties
- escalation routing

## 5. Implementation Coordination Support
QK Prima can handle:
- implementation planning support
- rollout coordination support
- support logistics coordination
- training/session arrangement support
- issue tracking administration
- PMO-type activities

## 6. Helpdesk Coordination Layer
QK Prima can handle:
- ticket administration support
- issue routing and escalation tracking
- SLA follow-up administration
- service review coordination
- operational reporting consolidation

## 7. Local Operational Support
QK Prima can handle:
- local presence support
- local engagement coordination
- site/workshop support if needed
- issue communication handling
- stakeholder facilitation support

## 8. Quality and Governance Administration
QK Prima can handle:
- document review coordination
- meeting minute management
- risk/action register administration
- progress reporting consolidation
- governance meeting support

## 9. Change Coordination
QK Prima can handle:
- change request administration
- approval routing
- tracking decisions
- version/change register maintenance
