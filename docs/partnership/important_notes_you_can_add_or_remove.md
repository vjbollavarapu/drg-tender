## Recommended Optional Clauses

### Scope Boundary Clause
Our responsibility is limited to the AWS cloud platform, runtime, deployment, security, monitoring, backup/recovery, and operational governance layers assigned to us.

### Application Responsibility Clause
Functional correctness of DRG logic, clinical coding rules, reimbursement rules, business calculations, and domain workflows remains the responsibility of the DRG solution owner.

### Third-Party Dependency Clause
Any issue arising from third-party services, government-managed infrastructure, external data sources, or upstream providers shall be handled on a reasonable-effort coordination basis unless explicitly assigned under this scope.

### Change Request Clause
Any material expansion of scope, environment count, resilience level, support window, or compliance obligations shall be subject to formal change control and commercial revision.
