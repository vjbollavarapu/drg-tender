# Negotiation Script
## For Discussion with DRG Solution Partner

Thank you for the opportunity to position ourselves as your cloud platform partner for this programme.

We see this as a strategic delivery partnership, not a narrow infrastructure assignment.

Your organisation clearly brings the DRG domain leadership, functional maturity, and subject-matter depth.  
Our role is to ensure that the solution can actually be run in a secure, resilient, observable, and supportable manner over the full 36-month lifecycle.

That distinction is important.

The tender scope itself is not limited to application development. It clearly expects:
- secure cloud hosting,
- database layers in cloud,
- API and SFTP integration,
- IAM and auditability,
- DevSecOps and CI/CD,
- helpdesk and SLA support,
- and successful progression through UAT, PAT and FAT into production operations.

That means the programme has a real platform engineering and service continuity layer which must be owned properly.

Our proposed figure is therefore not based on a one-time setup exercise.
It is based on taking responsibility across:
- architecture,
- deployment governance,
- security posture,
- monitoring and alerting,
- backup and recovery,
- operational continuity,
- production stability,
- and technical service governance over three years.

If the system experiences:
- availability issues,
- failed releases,
- performance degradation,
- security exposure,
- weak observability,
- or recovery failures,
the programme is affected regardless of whether the application itself is functionally complete.

That is the layer we are covering.

We are therefore proposing a single programme number, rather than a monthly support fee, because our role spans:
1. foundation and architecture,
2. implementation support,
3. integration and readiness,
4. go-live and stabilisation,
5. and sustained operations over the contract term.

Our pricing reflects the fact that we are becoming the AWS cloud platform partner to the programme, with continuity obligations and production accountability, not simply a deployment vendor.

If needed, we can adjust the figure only by adjusting one or more of the following:
- SLA expectations,
- operational depth,
- environment count,
- support window,
- or specific exclusions.

But if the expectation is that we remain responsible for ensuring the platform is up, secure, deployable, monitored, recoverable, and governable throughout the programme, then the proposed commercial position is fair and commercially justified.

Our objective is to support you as the DRG pioneer with a delivery model that strengthens the total bid, protects execution quality, and reduces operational risk throughout the three-year term.
