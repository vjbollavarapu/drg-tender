# Commercial Proposal
## AWS Cloud Platform Engineering, Security, Reliability and Operations
## National DRG System Programme
## Duration: 36 Months

## Commercial Summary
We propose a single programme figure for the full 36-month scope of AWS cloud platform engineering, deployment governance, security, monitoring, resilience, and operational continuity services required to support the National DRG System.

## Proposed Commercial Figure
**RM 1,050,000.00**

## Commercial Basis
The figure above is based on programme-level responsibility across:
- architecture accountability
- environment provisioning and control
- deployment and release governance
- security engineering and hardening
- platform observability and incident response
- backup and disaster recovery readiness
- performance and capacity engineering
- operational reporting and governance
- sustained continuity across implementation, stabilisation, rollout and ongoing operations

## Internal Commercial Logic
This is not an effort-only number.  
It reflects:
- continuity obligation over 36 months
- operational risk ownership
- production criticality
- dependency management complexity
- support for testing phases, go-live, and sustained run operations

## Suggested Payment Milestone Structure

### Option A – Milestone-Based Programme Billing
1. Contract commencement and architecture mobilisation – 15%
2. Foundation environments and baseline controls established – 15%
3. Build and CI/CD operational readiness completed – 15%
4. UAT/PAT readiness and pre-production controls completed – 15%
5. Production go-live and stabilisation completed – 15%
6. Year 2 operational continuity tranche – 12.5%
7. Year 3 operational continuity tranche – 12.5%

### Option B – Annualised Programme Billing
- Year 1: 40%
- Year 2: 30%
- Year 3: 30%

### Option C – Hybrid
- Initial mobilisation: 20%
- Implementation and go-live: 35%
- Remaining balance across operational governance and continuity milestones

## Commercial Assumptions
The proposed figure assumes:
- application solution partner retains ownership of application logic and DRG domain functionality
- AWS consumption charges are excluded
- third-party licensing and external audit costs are excluded
- government-controlled cloud or infrastructure outside our responsibility is excluded
- major scope expansion, additional environments, 24x7 on-site staffing, or materially different resilience obligations may require variation
