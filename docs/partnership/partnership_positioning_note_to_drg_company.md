# PARTNERSHIP POSITIONING NOTE TO DRG COMPANY

# Strategic Partnership Positioning Note
## AWS Cloud Platform, Reliability, Security and Operations Partner
### For the National DRG System Programme (36 Months)

## Purpose
This note defines our role as the AWS Cloud Platform and Operations Partner to support the successful delivery, secure operation, and sustained availability of the National DRG System over the full three-year programme lifecycle.

## Strategic Position
The DRG solution partner brings proven domain, clinical, grouping, costing, and national DRG subject-matter capability.  
Our role is to provide the cloud platform engineering, environment architecture, deployment governance, security hardening, observability, resilience, disaster recovery readiness, and operational continuity required to run the solution at production scale.

We are therefore not positioned as a generic infrastructure subcontractor.  
We are positioned as the partner responsible for ensuring that the solution is:
- securely deployed,
- consistently available,
- operationally governed,
- observable,
- scalable,
- recoverable,
- and supportable throughout the contract period.

## Why This Role Is Critical
The uploaded technical proposal describes a national-scale system with:
- seven inter-operating core modules,
- API and SFTP ingestion,
- integration with SMRP and MyGDX,
- multi-source patient-level data intake,
- cloud-hosted database layers,
- AI/EIS capabilities,
- auditability,
- RBAC/IAM,
- DevSecOps and CI/CD,
- Helpdesk and SLA operations,
- UAT, PAT and FAT gates,
- and high availability, security, compliance, and disaster recovery expectations.

This means platform failure, poor deployment governance, weak monitoring, weak security posture, or inadequate backup/recovery controls can materially jeopardise the programme even when the application itself is functionally strong.

Accordingly, our remit is to ensure the solution operates as a production-grade national platform and not merely as a deployed application.

## Our Position in the Delivery Model
The roles are separated as follows:

### DRG Solution Partner
- Functional ownership of the DRG solution
- DRG logic, grouping rules, costing logic, reporting logic
- Clinical and domain workflows
- Functional configuration and business acceptance support
- Subject matter engagement with stakeholders on DRG content

### Our Role
- AWS target architecture
- Environment design and provisioning
- Container platform and runtime operations
- Network security and connectivity model
- Identity, access, encryption, and secrets management
- CI/CD and release governance
- Monitoring, alerting, logging, and incident response
- Backup, recovery, and resilience engineering
- Production operations readiness
- Performance, capacity, and cloud governance
- Operational support coordination with the DRG solution partner

## Outcome We Commit To
We ensure the DRG platform is capable of being run as a secure, supportable, resilient, and observable service across the full lifecycle of implementation, stabilisation, rollout, and sustained operations.

## Engagement Horizon
This is a 36-month strategic partnership engagement, not a one-time implementation task.  
Our value is delivered across:
1. Foundation and architecture
2. Build and implementation support
3. Integration and stabilisation
4. Go-live and controlled rollout
5. Ongoing operations and service reliability
6. Continuous optimisation and governance
